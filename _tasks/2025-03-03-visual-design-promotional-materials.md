### **Goal**

Create compelling visual content and promotional materials that effectively communicate our autonomous sailboat project’s identity, technical achievements, and mission to various audiences.

### **Motivation**

Professional visual materials enhance our team’s presence at events, help attract new members and sponsors, and make complex technical information more accessible through effective visual communication.

### **Tasks & Activities**

- Design posters and flyers for recruitment events and competitions
- Create a cohesive PowerPoint template for team presentations
- Design branded merchandise concepts (t-shirts, stickers, etc.)
- Create visual assets for use on our website and social media
- Establish and maintain visual brand guidelines (colors, typography, logo usage)
- Design event banners and booth materials for competitions
- Create explanatory diagrams of boat systems for technical documentation
