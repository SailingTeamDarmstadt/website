### **Goal**

Enhance our team’s online presence by creating engaging social media content and managing our accounts to showcase our autonomous sailboat project to a wider audience.

### **Motivation**

Social media is crucial for documenting our journey, attracting new members, connecting with potential sponsors, and sharing our progress with the sailing and tech communities.

### **Tasks & Activities**

- Create and publish engaging posts across our social media platforms
- Capture photos and videos at team meetings, testing sessions, and competitions
- Produce short-form video content highlighting technical achievements and team culture
- Develop and maintain a consistent posting schedule
- Engage with followers and build community through comments and messages
- Track engagement metrics to identify what content resonates best
- Provide live updates from competitions and significant events
